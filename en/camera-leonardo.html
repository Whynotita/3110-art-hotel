Curiosity as an art form

Looking at the world
like no one before

Painter, engineer, anatomist, inventor: Leonardo da Vinci was above all a tireless observer. In Florence, where he trained in Verrocchio's workshop, he learned to look at nature with new eyes — and to turn wonder into knowledge.

The Deluxe room dedicated to him holds that same spirit. Harmonious lines, elegant finishes and plays of light create a refined, stimulating setting where the classic meets ingenuity. An invitation to slow down, observe and be surprised by the details.

Because, as the Master taught, “all our knowledge has its origin in our perceptions”: and here every sense finds its calm.
