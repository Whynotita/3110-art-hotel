Style, comfort, detail

A Florentine palazzo,
reinterpreted with elegance

31.10 Art Hotel combines style, comfort and design in a setting cared for down to the smallest detail. A short walk from the historic centre of Florence, cradle of the Renaissance, in the district that in the 19th century was home to Florentine artists, it enjoys an ideal position for those who wish to visit the city or are here on business.

The hotel spans two floors in a quiet residential area, minutes from the main sights yet outside the traffic-restricted zone of the historic centre. An eclectic design pairs refined furnishings with elegant finishes: 13 welcoming rooms, equipped with every service.

31 · 10 · 1737

A hymn to the beauty
of Florence

31.10 Art Hotel was born to celebrate an extraordinary date for the city: 31 October 1737. On that day Anna Maria Luisa de' Medici signed the “Family Pact”, the agreement by which she donated to Florence the artistic heritage collected over the centuries by the dynasty, stipulating that nothing could ever be removed from the city.

“The art was to remain in Florence — for the ornament of the State, for the benefit of the Public and to attract the curiosity of Foreigners.” One of our four themed rooms is dedicated to this gesture, a tribute to the figures who made Florence immortal.
