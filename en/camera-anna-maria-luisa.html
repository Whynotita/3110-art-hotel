The last Medici, guardian of Florence

Those who truly love a city
give it what they hold most precious

There is a love that does not ask to be remembered, yet changes a city's destiny forever. It is the love of Anna Maria Luisa de' Medici, last heir of a dynasty that made Florence the capital of beauty.

On 31 October 1737 she signed the “Family Pact”: she donated to the city the entire artistic heritage the Medici had gathered over three centuries, on one condition — that nothing should ever leave Florence. Thanks to her, the Uffizi, Palazzo Pitti and their collections are still here, for everyone.

Our Deluxe room is dedicated to this silent, immense gesture: an intimate and precious space where deep velvets, warm brass and soft light evoke the elegance of a court that knew how to turn wealth into shared beauty.
