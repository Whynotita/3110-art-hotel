Location

Home · Location
In an ideal position

In the heart of Florence,
away from the noise

31.10 Art Hotel is a short walk from Florence's historic centre, cradle of the Renaissance, in a quiet residential district. In a few minutes on foot you will reach the Giardino dell'Orticoltura and the Stibbert Museum; a few minutes more and you are in the heart of the city, among its masterpieces.

It is also the ideal choice for business travellers: the Fortezza da Basso and the Firenze Fiera congress centre are a short walk away.

How to find us

Easy from every direction

By train

From S. M. Novella station (about 20′): Via Nazionale to the ring road avenues, then Piazza della Libertà, Viale Matteotti and the first side street on the left, Via Frà Bartolommeo, no. 21. From Campo di Marte station (about 10′): along the railway to Via L. Giordano, then Via Frà Bartolommeo.

By car

From the motorway or the airport follow the ring road avenues, then the signs for Piazza della Libertà and Viale Don Minzoni towards “Cure”. At the third street on the right (Via Frà Maruffi) you will reach Via Frà Bartolommeo. The hotel can be reached by car, outside the ZTL.

By tram, taxi or plane

Florence Peretola airport is about 5.5 km away: by taxi it takes about twenty minutes (approximate cost around €20). Alternatively, the tramway T2 connects the airport to Piazza della Libertà, only 500 m from the hotel (a few minutes on foot).

Street parking

Possible on blue lines (paid, €2/hour from 8:00 am to 8:00 pm) and, where available, on white lines (free) in Via Frà Bartolommeo and nearby streets (Piazza Savonarola, Via Masaccio). Yellow lines are reserved for residents. Check street-cleaning days and times on the signs.

Garage parking

You can park at an affiliated garage (indicative rate €20 per 24h): contact reception directly to book.
