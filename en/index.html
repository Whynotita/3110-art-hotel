Welcome

An exclusive address
between art and hospitality

A short walk from the historic centre of Florence, cradle of the Renaissance, the 31.10 Art Hotel welcomes its guests to a residence where every detail is designed for absolute comfort. Elegant finishes and refined furnishings create a sophisticated, enveloping atmosphere.

Attentive, discreet service, an ideal position a short walk from the historic centre and the artistic legacy of the great Florentine masters make every stay a memorable experience.

Breakfast

Good morning
is a work of art

A rich buffet of sweet and savoury: cakes and pastries, yoghurt, fresh fruit, cheeses, juices and coffee. Breakfast at the 31.10 is a small ritual to enjoy slowly, before heading out to discover Florence.

In the elegant striped room or, on fine days, on the terrace wrapped in greenery: a cappuccino in the 31.10 branded cup, the scent of croissants and an intimate atmosphere, where every detail is cared for like in an art gallery.
